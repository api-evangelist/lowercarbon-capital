Walk into the Senra factory in Redondo Beach, and you’ll find former baristas and Uber drivers assembling wire harnesses for leading A&D companies. Eight weeks ago, some of them had never touched a soldering iron. Now they’re assembling one of the most critical building blocks of the electrified economy.
